Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. Applies to the Rotawise web dashboard, API and USSD service.

Who we are

Rotawise is an open source crop rotation decision support tool built by the Rotawise team for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. Questions about this policy: jerryjothan639@gmail.com.

What we collect

The dashboard does not use accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our servers keep short technical logs (time, page or API route requested, response code and response time) to keep the service running and secure. IP addresses are used in memory for rate limiting and are not written to logs.

When you use the USSD service, the mobile network and our USSD provider pass us your phone number and the menu choices you make. Rotawise uses the menu choices only to answer that session. We do not store or log your phone number, and we do not keep a history of your sessions.

Farm locations in Rotawise are pilot locations chosen by the team. We do not collect farmers' names, addresses or field boundaries.

Data sources

Advice is computed from public Earth observation data (NASA HLS, NASA POWER, NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6), ESA WorldCover and ISRIC SoilGrids (both CC BY 4.0). These datasets describe land and climate, not people.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Our hosting and USSD providers process requests on our behalf only to deliver the service.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we do not keep phone numbers or accounts, in most cases we hold nothing that identifies you. Contact us at the address above.

Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. The service applies rate limiting, strict security headers and input validation.

Children

Rotawise is intended for adult farmers and extension agents.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date.
